WorkingOptimal Transport for a Multiclass G/GI/N + GI Queue

Haoyu Wu, Bahar Taşkesen, Amy Ward

Queueing Scheduling · Optimal Transport · Fluid Limits

Work in progress — happy to discuss by email.

Abstract

We propose a new approach to the multiclass many-server queueing scheduling problem in the fluid limit, inspired by optimal transport theory. The reformulation yields the optimal scheduling policy within a general class of discipline scheduling policies over a finite horizon.

ConferenceDynamic Competition in Elections: A Noncooperative Game of Candidate Positioning

Haoyu Wu, Ao Tang

Game Theory · Dynamic Game · Optimal Control

Accepted, Conference on Decision and Control (CDC), 2026

Abstract

A noncooperative dynamical model of elections is developed. We analyze the case of two candidates competing for a fixed, stationary electorate over a finite horizon. Equilibrium candidate trajectories are shown to be qualitatively determined by the interaction of three factors: voter distribution, campaign duration, and initial positioning.

JournalAn Even Tighter Bound for the Shapley–Folkman–Starr Theorem

Haoyu Wu, Ao Tang

Nonconvex Optimization · Error Estimation · Shapley–Folkman Lemma

Journal of Mathematical Economics, vol. 114 (2024)

Paper DOI

Abstract

Refines the classical Shapley–Folkman–Starr lemma to obtain a tighter estimate of the Hausdorff distance between a Minkowski sum and its convex hull, by treating all components jointly rather than separately. Applies the result to the course-allocation problem, a combinatorial assignment setting.
